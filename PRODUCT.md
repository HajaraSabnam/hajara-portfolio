# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Anyone who looks Hajara up: recruiters and hiring managers for AI/ML roles, researchers and professors, colleagues and clients met through Elchai Group, and friends from hackathons and LinkedIn. Most arrive from a link (LinkedIn, CV, email signature), skim on a laptop or phone for one or two minutes, and want to understand who Hajara is and how good the work is.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Hajara Sabnam, AI Engineer at Elchai Group. It exists to make Hajara's research and engineering work credible at a glance and to show a little of the person behind it. Success: a visitor leaves knowing what Hajara has published and built, trusts the quality of that work, and has an easy way to get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious, credible. Professional first, for a working engineer, with a quiet personal layer (real photos, first-person voice) rather than playful decoration. Confidence comes from the work itself, stated plainly.

## Anti-references

- "Vibe-coded" / AI-template sites: glowing status pills, gradient text, shadcn and Vercel starter looks, big-number stat rows, cursor spotlights, word-by-word heading reveals, mono uppercase kickers.
- Luxury or hospitality aesthetics ("hotel website"): light italic display serifs, plum and gold, ornate quotes.
- Over-playful scrapbook styling: tilted polaroids, tape, handwritten doodles everywhere, spinning stickers.
- Loud saturated accents (bright orange) and heavy outlined display type.

## Design Principles

1. Let the work speak: research results, venues and awards carry the page; decoration never competes with them.
2. Personal, not performative: soul comes from real photos, first-person words and real moments, not from gimmicks.
3. Quiet confidence: plain section names, restrained color, generous space; one clear anchor per section.
4. Motion with purpose: gentle reveals and functional transitions only; nothing that loops for its own sake except slow, pausable photo and topic strips.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus states, full keyboard access for filters, accordions and the image viewer. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling looping and entrance animation.
